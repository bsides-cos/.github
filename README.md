## Welcome

Thank you for your interest in Security B-Sides Colorado Springs (BSidesCOS). We are a **501(c)(3) nonprofit** based in Colorado, proud to be part of the global B-Sides community.

B-Sides events combine security expertise from a variety of platforms in search of the "next big thing" in information security. B-Sides is an open platform that gives security experts and industry professionals the opportunity to share ideas, insights, and develop longstanding relationships with others in the community.

Although attendance is **free**, the organizers shoulder the cost of hosting each event. We rely on the generosity of sponsors and volunteers to make it happen.

---

## About Security B-Sides

Security B-Sides is a framework of events that enables individuals to expand the spectrum of conversation beyond the confines of traditional media. B-Sides is an **"unconference"** that follows the **open spaces** format — ranging from structured presentations to smaller breakout groups — providing direct connection between speakers and the audience.

B-Sides events are:

- 🌐 **Community organized** — driven by local individuals
- 🙋 **Volunteer driven** — built by people who care
- 🏢 **Corporate funded** — supported by sponsors who believe in the community
- 🆓 **Free to attend** — accessible to everyone

## Goals

B-Sides acts as a complement to current events by enabling long-tail spectrum conversations that go beyond mainstream topics. Our goal is to **foster communication and collaboration** while increasing the depth of conversation.

Where major conferences focus on current hot topics, B-Sides events explore **the fringe** — from hardware hacking to gender issues, from social media hacking to proximity-based identity theft. B-Sides gives voice to the conversation happening just below the surface.

## Target Audience

BSidesCOS brings together:

- 🔐 Information security professionals
- 💡 Industry and thought leaders
- 🎓 Students eager to grow their knowledge
- 🛠️ Makers, hackers, and researchers from all sectors and backgrounds

Unlike traditional conferences, B-Sides provides a **small and intimate environment** for attendees to directly engage speakers before, during, and after their talks. Attendees are looking for more than information — they're looking to make connections they'll rely on throughout the year.

## Our Ethos

### Frameworks

Traditional media and physical events constrain themselves with space, time, and existing narratives. B-Sides removes those barriers by providing an open framework — more speakers, more topics, more events. What started as a single event in Las Vegas has grown into a global movement spanning 62+ countries.

### Participation

B-Sides is not made up of *members*, but **participants**. Every person who shows up brings something to contribute — hardware, organizational skills, knowledge, or simply their presence. Sponsors bring resources that help fuel the conversation and are directly connected with attendees who matter to their mission.
